# Roadmap

## Vision
A collection of small, dependency-light command-line tools that a Turkish
university student actually uses day to day: money, studying, files and PDFs.
Every tool lives in `tools/<name>/` with its own README, code, tests and
examples, and is finished to a usable v1 before the next one starts.
